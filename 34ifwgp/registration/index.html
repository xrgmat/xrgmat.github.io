Registration

Important dates

  • Deadline for registration: July 15, 2026. Click here to register.

    The registration fee is 150€ for master and PhD students and 350€ for professors, with an early bird of 300€.

  • Deadline for submitting abstracts for contributed talks: June 25, 2026.

  • Deadline for submitting abstracts for posters: July 15, 2026.

    Send your title and abstract to 34ifwgp(at)gmail.com after registering. Only a limited number of contributed talks will be accepted. The scientific committee will select them after the deadline date.

In the event that the conference organizers manage transportation or lodging, the organizing committee will personally handle these aspects and abstain from employing the services of any travel agency. In case of doubt do not hesitate to contact us (34ifwgp(at)gmail.com).
