The International Fall Workshops on Geometry and Physics have been held annually since 1992, uniting Spanish and Portuguese geometers and physicists, along with a growing number of international participants. These meetings provide a unique forum for the exchange of ideas across fields such as Differential Geometry, Applied Mathematics, and Theoretical Physics. A key feature of the IFWGP is the active involvement of enthusiastic young researchers, fostering collaboration and innovation in the mathematical sciences.
